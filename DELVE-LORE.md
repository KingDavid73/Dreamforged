# Lore direction: Fa-Nuit-Hen and the Unending Dream

Fa-Nuit-Hen is named in Morrowind's own 36 Lessons of Vivec, Sermon 1,
where he is associated with martial motions and the Barons of Move Like This.
ESO develops him as the Demiprince of Maelstrom, a realm of martial challenges.
These establish a better foundation for repeatable combat trials than a random
physical dungeon inexplicably extending under Seyda Neen.

Our original fiction: Nerethi is a herald in a Dunmer guise, offering passage
to the Unending Dream, a proving ground associated with Fa-Nuit-Hen. The refuge,
herald, broker, coins, and endlessly descending dungeon are mod inventions.
We are not reproducing the Maelstrom Arena or claiming that this exact location
or herald appears in canon. No ESO dialogue or assets are imported.

Gameplay rationale: floor themes are shapes borrowed by the realm, guardians
are trials, portal return is permitted by its host, and the broker trades in
forging coins because earned proofs matter there more than Tamrielic gold.
